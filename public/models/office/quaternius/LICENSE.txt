men.glb and women.glb each contain one shared skeleton, the pack's 24
animation clips, and the modular pieces (head, torso, legs, feet). The
office combines head, torso and legs per agent seed and reuses those
clips for every character of that gender. Feet follow the leg outfit.

Geometry is compressed with meshoptimizer (EXT_meshopt_compression,
KHR_mesh_quantization). There is no Draco payload. Sizes: men.glb about
2.2 MB for every male part and all 24 clips; women.glb about 1.8 MB.
A single assembled character reuses that library, so the unique mesh
payload stays under 1 MB.

The 24 clips are Death, Gun_Shoot, HitRecieve, HitRecieve_2, Idle,
Idle_Gun, Idle_Gun_Pointing, Idle_Gun_Shoot, Idle_Neutral, Idle_Sword,
Interact, Kick_Left, Kick_Right, Punch_Left, Punch_Right, Roll, Run,
Run_Back, Run_Left, Run_Right, Run_Shoot, Sword_Slash, Walk and Wave.
There is no sitting clip. Mixamo's seated animation was not imported:
downloading it requires an Adobe account. The desk pose is a rotation
on the shared skeleton (UpperLeg, LowerLeg, Foot, Abdomen).

Clothing materials are tinted with the earthy office palette
(lib/office-aparencia.ts). Skin, eyes, eyebrows and hair keep the
colors modeled by Quaternius.
